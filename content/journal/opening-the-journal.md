Most designers' websites are portfolios: a grid of finished projects, each photographed on its best day. The Scribble Lab already has a home for that kind of record. This site is built for something else.

**Still Drawing** is a journal about Nashemman Sahiba Zargar and the life around the work: where she comes from, how her practice took shape, what she is building next, and the travel, conversations and ideas that feed into all of it.

## How it will grow

Entries here will come in a few forms:

- **Travel stories:** places seen with a designer's eye.
- **Process notes:** how an idea becomes a drawing, and a drawing becomes a built thing.
- **Observations:** small things noticed and worth keeping.
- **Ideas:** thoughts that haven't found their project yet.

Nothing is published here until Nashemman has written or approved it. Some pages, like talks and the new venture, are deliberately open for now. They are chapters waiting for their material, not missing pieces.

The line is still being drawn. This is where to watch it.
