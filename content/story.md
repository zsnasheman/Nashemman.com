## Roots {#roots | Kashmir}

The story starts in Kashmir. It would be easy to open with the postcard version, all lakes and snowlines, and this profile chooses not to. A place that shapes a designer rarely shows up as a view. It shows up in habits of attention: what someone notices in a room, what they think is worth making by hand, how much patience they give a detail.

Which of those Kashmir gave Nashemman is hers to say, and this chapter leaves room for her to say it.

## Schooling {#schooling | Delhi · Dubai}

She studied at Delhi Public School and went on to complete her bachelor's degree at Amity University Dubai. That move matters to the shape of the story. It placed her, early, in a city where design is an everyday public act: new interiors, shopfronts and event spaces appear, change and disappear on a timetable few other places keep.

## Practice {#practice | Dubai}

Before there was a studio with her name on the door, there were roughly five years of working inside other practices, among them XBD, Swiss Bureau and DLR Group. This profile keeps the detail light on purpose: no titles, no timeline. What those years offer a designer is less a list of credits than an education in how studios actually run, how ideas survive budgets, and how a drawing becomes a built thing on a deadline.

## The studio {#studio | The Scribble Lab, 2022}

In 2022 she founded [The Scribble Lab](https://thescribblelab.com/), a creative design and build studio. The pairing is the point. Design and build under one roof means the person who draws the idea stays close to the people who make it, across residential and commercial interiors, brand activations, kinetic window displays, exhibitions and events.

That range, from a family home to a window that moves, says something about the practice. It is interested in space that performs: rooms that have to work every day, and moments that only have to work for a week but need to be unforgettable while they do.
