## Five years in practice {#practice | Practice | practice}

She then spent about five years in design practice, at companies including XBD, Swiss Bureau and DLR Group, before starting a studio of her own.

## The Scribble Lab {#studio | Founder, 2022 | board}

In 2022 she founded [The Scribble Lab](https://thescribblelab.com/), a creative design and build studio. It works across residential and commercial interiors, brand activations, kinetic window displays, exhibitions and events.

Design and build under one roof means the idea and its making stay in the same hands, and she leads both.

## What comes next {#next | In development | future}

She is developing a second business, focused on interior accessories. Its name and launch will follow.
