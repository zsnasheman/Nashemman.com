The Scribble Lab is the chapter where Nashemman's practice became her own. She founded it in 2022 as a **design and build** studio, and that combination shapes everything it does. The drawing and the making sit under one roof, so an idea does not have to be handed across a table and hoped for.

The work moves between very different lengths of time. A home or an office has to hold up to years of ordinary days. A brand activation, an exhibition stand or an event has a few days, sometimes a few hours, to make its point. Kinetic window displays sit somewhere in between: fixed in a frame, but built to move.

This page is a glimpse, not a catalogue. The studio's own website holds its projects, services and enquiries.
